HOW IT WORKS

The Admission Process

A seamless 6-step journey from initial inquiry to academic excellence.

Free Assessment

We begin with a comprehensive skill gap analysis to understand the student's current academic standing and specific areas for improvement.

Evaluation

Our academic experts meticulously review the assessment results to identify strengths, weaknesses, and the student's unique learning styles.

Recommendation

Based on the evaluation, we design a customized curriculum path tailored specifically to the student's personal academic goals.

Tutor Match

We carefully pair the student with an ideal mentor whose teaching style perfectly aligns with the student's learning profile.

Batch Selection

Families can choose from a variety of flexible timings and batch sizes (1-on-1 or small group) to fit their busy schedules.

Enrollment

Welcome to BrightPath! The student is now officially enrolled and ready to embark on their journey to academic excellence.
